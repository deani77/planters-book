# Platform parity

Android and iOS share the fictional `sample-data/architecture-parity.json` contract fixture. Both test suites must agree on field identifiers, ordered field overview, preservation of legacy identifiers and planting dates, separate age/ABW records, measured zero, yield and annual achievement. Add regression fixtures to both suites when report semantics change.

Field Details owns ABW, source profile/block facts and ordered field information. Upkeep offers three estate operation summaries then field selection with six categories. Estate manuring totals must not be assigned to a field. Data-entry controls stay out of read-only reports. Changes to Excel/JSON/ZIP input must specify acceptance or rejection on both platforms; unsupported populated sheets must not be silently omitted.

PR #20’s four crop sections and PR #21’s proposed workbook are separate drafts. Their equivalent iOS implementations must be reviewed before marking those capabilities as cross-platform complete. This change aligns the merged field/upkeep behavior and establishes shared fixtures; it does not claim those two drafts are released.
